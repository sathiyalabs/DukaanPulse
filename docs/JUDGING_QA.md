# Jury answers and pilot hypotheses

| Question | Answer grounded in this prototype |
|---|---|
| Why AI? | Multilingual speech and interpretation can reduce the effort of understanding a receipt and correcting context. The deterministic rules remain authoritative. Sarvam classifies evidence-question intent into a strict focus schema; code supplies the figures and explanatory templates. AI is useful at the language interface, not required to calculate a median. |
| Why would Paytm add this? | A potential workflow after checking collections connects a signal to an owner-approved experiment and later review. The pilot would test whether it improves comprehension and provides a reason to return. No retention gain is proven. |
| How does this differ from existing insights? | The demonstrated distinction is a linked decision record: source dates, correctable context, bounded economics, exact approval, real printable material and saved outcome. It is a product hypothesis, not a measured claim that Paytm or competitors lack those capabilities. |
| Where do the numbers come from? | Uploaded CSV records pass the same validator as generated synthetic samples. Completed values, counts, averages and window counts are code-computed. Earlier same-weekday eligible periods produce the median. Each receipt snapshots the calculation and context. |
| What if data is incomplete? | Coverage is unknown until confirmed. Early closure makes a full-window comparison invalid. Fewer than three historical periods prevents supported offer advice. Closed and open-with-zero are distinct. The owner can still choose investigation without a consumer offer. |
| Can the app prove an offer caused growth? | No. It shows arithmetic against the original and saved baseline, and asks about coverage, hours, actual execution and exceptional events. Multiple confounders remain. A controlled pilot design would be needed for stronger causal claims. |
| What is live? | The implemented app validates actual files, computes evidence, revises context, approves terms, stores in D1, generates multilingual posters and saves follow-up outcomes. Browser refresh persistence and the synthetic journey were tested in the managed preview. |
| What is simulated or pending? | All demonstration records and the shop are synthetic. Sarvam adapters are implemented and tested with mocks, but live TTS/STT/chat and account-credit details await an authorised credential. There is no production Paytm integration, customer messaging or payment execution. |
| How are usage and costs controlled? | Analytics and fixed-language text never call AI. Requests are explicit, bounded and have a 20-second timeout without automatic retries. Revision/language/model/voice caches and single-flight claims prevent unnecessary work. Daily application caps are 12 per workspace and 100 per deployment. They are not Sarvam balance figures. |
| What does the 500-credit allocation mean? | It is the team's reported organiser allocation. Its billing unit, remaining balance, expiry and service eligibility have not been verified. No conversion to rupees, requests or minutes is assumed. |
| Why three languages? | Owners can read the same canonical receipt in English, Hindi or Tamil. Fixed copy is dictionary-based. This demonstrates language access but still needs native-speaker usability and pronunciation testing. |
| How does it learn? | It remembers owner corrections, rejected actions, preferences and past outcomes in the saved history. It does not claim its prediction accuracy improves. There is no evaluated demand predictor. |
| Does a combo increase customer count? | Not necessarily. Payment count and average payment value are separate goals. The app never equates a bundle with additional customers or predicted conversions. |
| Is the discount profitable? | The app checks contribution per redemption before fixed costs, stock limits, budget and the owner-entered floor. Feasibility does not establish overall profitability or sufficient demand. |
| How is data isolated? | Private hosting scopes records to the signed-in owner. Anonymous managed preview uses a random HttpOnly session cookie. Production multi-merchant roles, data retention, backups and operational security require additional work before rollout. |

## Proposed pilot, not completed research

Start with five discovery interviews across tea/snack shops. Observe what owners currently do after checking collections and whether the problem matters. Test the same receipt, coverage correction and offer decision tasks in each preferred language. Ask owners to explain both the signal and its limits without prompting.

If that supports the hypothesis, run a small opt-in multi-week pilot with approved aggregate payment access. Define the denominator for every metric before collecting data:

- Understanding: owners correctly describe the comparison, missing context and non-causal limit / owners completing the task.
- Completed experiments: experiments confirmed ran and later reviewed / approved experiments, preserving stops and skips.
- Return to review: owners returning within the agreed review period / owners whose review is due.
- Report time-to-completion, assistance required, coverage failures, language errors, negative outcomes and reasons for abandonment.

Avoid evaluating only positive examples. Compare language task completion and review behaviour to the merchant's existing process if feasible. Longer matched histories and a controlled evaluation are needed before claims about incremental growth.

## Paytm and commercial fit

A successful pilot could become an approved feature within Paytm for Business or an approved voice surface. That requires partnership, consent, data access, account isolation, security review and measured utility. Proposed integration architecture would import authorised aggregate summaries, retain the Decision Receipt contract and return saved experiment outcomes to the approved surface.

A free core receipt with a per-outlet paid workflow, or partner-funded distribution, are pricing hypotheses. There is no validated price, willingness to pay, traction, monetisation result or partnership approval. Test commercial interest only after usefulness and repeat review are established.
